133 members in 37 countries work on open digital reading

EDRLab is a network of publishers, ebook and audiobook distributors, booksellers, libraries and developers of reading applications. Members use and shape Readium, Readium LCP and Thorium Reader, follow the evolution of EPUB through EDRLab's W3C membership, and meet at the Digital Publishing Summit.

What membership brings, by profile

  • Publishers and content producers

    Get advice from EDRLab's Head of Accessibility on producing accessible EPUB files. Use Qualebook, a quality reference framework for digital books, and LINA25, a set of resources in French that prepares the book chain for the European Accessibility Act. Protect your titles with Readium LCP.

    See membership terms

  • Distributors and booksellers

    Deploy Readium LCP to distribute protected ebooks and audiobooks, and test your catalogues with the reading applications of other members. Exhibit at the London Book Fair and the Frankfurt Book Fair on the Members Collective Booth at a reduced price.

    See membership terms

  • Libraries

    Offer your patrons Thorium Reader, the free reading application that many libraries already use, with a profile customized for your institution. Lend and read protected content with Readium LCP.

    See membership terms

  • Reading app developers and technology providers

    Build on Readium, the open-source toolkit for reading digital publications, and implement Readium LCP so that your application opens protected content. Present your use cases to the board and the Members Circle.

    See membership terms

What every member receives

  • A page in the online member directory, which you keep up to date
  • Access to the Members Circle, a monthly webinar for members, and to the working groups
  • Advice from EDRLab's Head of Accessibility
  • A reduced-price exhibitor space on the Members Collective Booth at the London Book Fair and the Frankfurt Book Fair
  • A feature in the monthly newsletter, which reaches over 1,000 industry professionals
  • Discounted member rates at the Digital Publishing Summit
  • A vote in the election of the Board of Directors
