ENACA, for Enhancing Accessibility in Comic Albums, makes digital comics, manga and webtoons accessible with computer vision and language models. The project is led by La Rochelle University (L3i) and Mangas.io. EDRLab contributes through the evolution of EPUB and of the Divina format.
