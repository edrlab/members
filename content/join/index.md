benefits:
  - icon: globe
    title: Open ebook ecosystem
    text: Work with other members on interoperable, open solutions for ebooks and audiobooks.
  - icon: key-round
    title: Readium and Readium LCP
    text: Use and contribute to the Readium toolkits, and deploy Readium LCP for protected EPUB and audiobooks.
  - icon: monitor
    title: Thorium Reader
    text: Follow and influence the roadmap of Thorium Reader, the free desktop reading application.
  - icon: accessibility
    title: EPUB and accessibility
    text: Follow the evolution of EPUB and accessibility standards through EDRLab's W3C membership.
  - icon: users
    title: Members Circle and working groups
    text: Take part in the Members Circle and in thematic working groups.
  - icon: building-2
    title: Governance
    text: Vote at the general assembly and stand for election to the board.
  - icon: calendar
    title: Network and events
    text: Meet other members at the Digital Publishing Summit and at member events.
dues_title: Annual dues
dues_note: Membership becomes effective on the date of payment of the membership dues. Payment options currently include bank transfer, credit card, PayPal, or cheque (in France). First-year dues are payable at the time of application.
dues_intro: Dues depend on the annual budget or revenue of the organization. They are invoiced once a year.
