The Members Collective Booth is a shared EDRLab booth at the London Book Fair and the Frankfurt Book Fair. Members can book an exhibitor space on it at a reduced price. Past editions of the Digital Publishing Summit are available as videos on the [EDRLab website](https://www.edrlab.org/category/events/).
