faqs:
- q: Who can become a member of EDRLab?
  a: 'Any organization with an activity related to digital publishing: publishers, ebook and audiobook distributors, booksellers, libraries, developers of reading applications and technology providers.'
- q: How much does membership cost?
  a: Dues range from €500 to €20,000 per year, excluding tax, depending on revenue or budget and on size. See the dues table on the [Join page](/join/#dues).
- q: When does membership start?
  a: On the date the membership dues are paid. First-year dues are payable at the time of application.
- q: How can I pay?
  a: By bank transfer, credit card or PayPal, or by cheque in France.
- q: What is Readium LCP and who uses it?
  a: Readium LCP is a non-proprietary DRM managed by EDRLab. Publishers, distributors and libraries use it to protect and lend publications, and reading applications implement it to open protected content. Any member can use it.
- q: How is EDRLab connected to the W3C?
  a: EDRLab is a W3C member. Through it, members receive feedback on the work of the W3C Publishing Working Group and can present their ideas to the group.
- q: What accessibility support do members receive?
  a: Members can ask EDRLab's Head of Accessibility for advice. EDRLab also publishes [Qualebook](https://edrlab.github.io/qualebook/en/), a quality reference framework for digital books, and [LINA25](https://www.lina25.fr/), a set of resources in French on the European Accessibility Act.
- q: What is the Members Circle?
  a: The Members Circle is a monthly webinar open to all EDRLab members.
- q: What is the Members Collective Booth?
  a: It is a shared EDRLab booth at the London Book Fair and the Frankfurt Book Fair. Members can book an exhibitor space on it at a reduced price.
- q: Does membership include the Digital Publishing Summit?
  a: Members attend at discounted rates. Tickets are not included in the dues.
- q: How does my organization appear in the member directory, and how do I update it?
  a: Each member has a page with its logo, description, country, activity and a link to its website. To update your entry, submit a pull request on the public GitHub repository [edrlab/members](https://github.com/edrlab/members). The README describes each field.
