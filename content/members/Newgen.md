  <h2>About Newgen KnowledgeWorks</h2>

  <p>
    Newgen KnowledgeWorks helps publishers turn content into
    high-quality, accessible digital publications. We combine
    deep publishing expertise with conversion technology and
    global production capacity, supporting everything from new
    EPUB releases to large-scale backlist digitisation.
  </p>

  <p>
    Our work connects content creation, production and delivery.
    We help publishers build structured, reusable content,
    improve the reading experience and unlock new value from
    their collections.
  </p>

  <p>Our core digital capabilities include:</p>

  <ul>
    <li>
      <strong>EPUB and ebook production:</strong>
      Conversion and production for new titles and backlists,
      including complex academic, educational and illustrated content.
    </li>

    <li>
      <strong>XML and content conversion:</strong>
      Structured content that supports digital delivery, reuse
      and efficient publication across multiple formats.
    </li>

    <li>
      <strong>Accessibility:</strong>
      Accessible EPUBs and PDFs, remediation and image descriptions,
      with accessibility built into production workflows.
    </li>

    <li>
      <strong>Digital publishing platforms:</strong>
      Our NOVA platform helps publishers host, distribute and sell
      books, journals and collections, building direct relationships
      with readers and institutions.
    </li>

    <li>
      <strong>Workflow and automation:</strong>
      Publishing technology backed by experienced teams, helping
      publishers manage volume, complexity and demanding schedules.
    </li>
  </ul>

  <p>
    Beyond digital production, we provide a complete publishing
    service: commissioning and content development, editorial,
    design and illustration, typesetting, project management,
    localisation, technical documentation and print manufacturing
    support.
  </p>

  <p>
    We work with independent publishers and global publishing
    groups across academic, journals, education, ELT, trade,
    legal and professional publishing. Whether the brief is to
    convert a collection, develop a new digital product or manage
    an entire publishing programme, we bring the people, processes
    and technology to deliver it.
  </p>
