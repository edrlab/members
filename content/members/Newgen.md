<h2>About Newgen KnowledgeWorks</h2>
<p>Newgen KnowledgeWorks partners with publishers to deliver digital publishing solutions and services across the publishing lifecycle. Its expertise combines content production, technology, and workflow services to help publishers prepare and distribute high-quality digital content.</p>
<h3>Our services</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>EPUB and ebook production:</strong> Creating and converting digital books for different publishing requirements and reading platforms.</li>
<li><strong>XML and content conversion:</strong> Transforming content into structured, reusable digital formats for publishing workflows.</li>
<li><strong>Accessibility:</strong> Supporting the production of accessible digital publications to help make content available to a wider range of readers.</li>
<li><strong>NOVA digital publishing platform:</strong> Providing technology to support digital publishing and content workflows.</li>
<li><strong>Workflow and automation:</strong> Helping publishers improve production processes through workflow optimisation and automation.</li>
<li><strong>Wider publishing services:</strong> Supporting publishers with services across the content development and publishing lifecycle.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Industries served</h3>
<p>Newgen KnowledgeWorks works with publishers and organisations across the publishing ecosystem, supporting digital content production and publishing technology needs.</p>
