summary: "Newgen KnowledgeWorks
Newgen KnowledgeWorks helps publishers turn content into high-quality, accessible digital publications. We combine deep publishing expertise with conversion technology and global production capacity, supporting everything from new EPUB releases to large-scale backlist digitisation.

Our work connects content creation, production and delivery. We help publishers build structured, reusable content, improve the reading experience and unlock new value from their collections.

Our core digital capabilities include:

EPUB and ebook production: Conversion and production for new titles and backlists, including complex academic, educational and illustrated content.
XML and content conversion: Structured content that supports digital delivery, reuse and efficient publication across multiple formats.
Accessibility: Accessible EPUBs and PDFs, remediation and image descriptions, with accessibility built into production workflows.
Digital publishing platforms: Our NOVA platform helps publishers host, distribute and sell books, journals and collections, building direct relationships with readers and institutions.
Workflow and automation: Publishing technology backed by experienced teams, helping publishers manage volume, complexity and demanding schedules.
Beyond digital production, we provide a complete publishing service: commissioning and content development, editorial, design and illustration, typesetting, project management, localisation, technical documentation and print manufacturing support.

We work with independent publishers and global publishing groups across academic, journals, education, ELT, trade, legal and professional publishing. Whether the brief is to convert a collection, develop a new digital product or manage an entire publishing programme, we bring the people, processes and technology to deliver it.
