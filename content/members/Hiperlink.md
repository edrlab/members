summary: "Hiperlink, with its experienced team of experts, connects users with information resources available in print and electronic formats. Since its inception, Hiperlink has focused on customer satisfaction , utilizing technology at the highest level quickly and securely, developing its infrastructure accordingly, and training its personnel in this direction.

Publishing: Hiperlink, which also undertakes ambitious work in the field of publishing, has a publishing policy that respects intellectual capital and aims to protect the rights of authors. In line with this, it has contributed more than 500 publications to Turkish literature to date.
Electronic (Digital Library): Embracing the vision of transmitting and preserving cultural memory for future generations, Hiperkitap has brought more than 20,000 publications to the digital library environment under its brand.
Distribution: We professionally distribute information and cultural resources such as books and DVDs published in Turkey to institutions and individuals both domestically and internationally.
Literature review: We continuously conduct literature reviews of information and cultural resources published in Turkey, prepare the most up-to-date lists, and submit them to relevant institutions.
Turnkey Library Projects: Hiperlink provides architectural design, automation systems, shelving, furniture, tools, and equipment for newly established libraries, information centers, and archives, creating ready-to-use, turnkey solutions.
It carries out activities and services such as modernization of existing libraries and information centers, collection updates, and technological upgrades.
Cataloging: Our firm, with its professional library experience, catalogs information resources in all formats (books, journals, DVDs, etc.) for institutions according to MARC, RDA, Dublin Core, and Mods standards, using Dewey and LC classification systems. We also handle all library technical processes (spine labeling, barcoding, stamping, etc.), delivering the materials ready for shelving.
Project Preparation and Consulting: Within the framework of the European Union Information Policy, various projects are prepared by expert staff in the fields of cultural studies, libraries, information centers, archives, and documentation centers."
