summary: "<p>Founded more than 50 years ago by the French Publishers Association (Syndicat national de l’édition – SNE), ASFORED is France’s leading organization dedicated to education and professional development for the publishing industry.

<p>Each year, ASFORED trains more than 250 apprentices across the entire book value chain and provides continuing education to over 1,500 publishing professionals, helping companies and individuals adapt to the ongoing transformation of publishing professions and content industries.

<p>Beyond France, ASFORED develops dedicated international programs and partnerships with universities, professional organizations, and institutions in Europe, Asia, and North America. Through these collaborations, ASFORED promotes knowledge exchange, innovation, and the dissemination of best practices to support the future of publishing in an increasingly digital and interconnected world."
