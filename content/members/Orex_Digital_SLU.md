We deploy, migrate and maintain Koha ILS for more than 90 academic, public and special libraries across Spain and Latin America. OREX holds ISO 9001:2015 certification and is certified under the Spanish National Security Framework (ENS Grado Medio, 2026). Our current focus is the development of a digital lending platform — concept similar to eBiblio or OverDrive — built on Readium LCP and designed for the needs of public and academic libraries running Koha. The platform features patron authentication via Koha REST/SIP2, atomic one-copy/one-user availability control, four license models, GDPR-compliant patron pseudonymization, and a dedicated firewalled LCP Server deployment.
